## Client Feature List

The client requested the following website features:

-   Professional website that shows quality, tradition and a family
    feel.
-   Home page with brand story and "Since 1999" legacy.
-   About Us / Our Journey timeline with the founder, team and new
    branch.
-   Quotes and real customer testimonials.
-   Dynamic frame gallery: staff upload frame photos and they appear
    automatically, with men / women / kids / sunglasses filters and
    brand and price tags.
-   Lens uploads by the shop.
-   Eye camps section, upcoming and past, managed by the admin.
-   Branch locator with map, hours and phone.
-   Contact / enquiry form with WhatsApp and click-to-call.
-   Book an eye test form.
-   Virtual try-on (research, later module).
