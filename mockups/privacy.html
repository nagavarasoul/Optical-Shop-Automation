How we use it

Only for the reason you gave it to us: to manage your booking or answer your message. We don't sell your details, use them for advertising or send you marketing messages. If we ever want to send reminders or offers, we'll ask you first.

  • Our staff. Only signed-in VS Lens Mart staff can see bookings and messages.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what details we hold about you;
  • correct or complete them;
  • delete them;
  • stop using them. Withdrawing your consent doesn't affect anything we did before you asked.

You can also name someone to act for you if you're unable to.

How to ask: WhatsApp or call either branch, email us, or visit a branch (see Contact us). We'll reply within 30 days. DUMMY (C23) If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Parents and guardians can book an eye test for a child using their own name and number. Please don't send us a child's details through the website beyond what's needed for the booking.

Keeping it safe

The website uses a secure connection (HTTPS). Our forms can only add a booking or a message; nobody outside our staff can read them back. Staff sign in to see them, and we remove access when someone leaves.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date. Big changes, such as adding visitor statistics, will be shown on the website before they happen.
