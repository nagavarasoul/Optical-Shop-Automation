How to review

These are designs of every page, so you can see and try the website before we build it. Nothing here is live, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

  • 1. Look on your phone and computerMost customers will use a phone. Tap the menu, buttons and filters as a customer would.
  • 2. Yellow dashed notes are for youThey explain what's missing or how something will work. The "Review notes" button at the bottom left hides them, so you can see the page as customers will.
  • 3. "DUMMY" means a placeholderPrices, brands, reviews, photos and some numbers are examples. We'll replace every one with your real details before launch.
  • 4. Light and dark modeThe sun / moon button at the top switches the whole site. Please look at both.
  • 5. Mark each page belowChoose "Looks good" or "Needs changes", write any notes, then tap "Send feedback on WhatsApp" at the bottom.

Decisions we made: please confirm

Where your answers were open or pointed in different directions, we chose a simple option so the work could continue. Tell us if you'd like any of these done differently.

  1. The shop system comes after the website. Customer records, prescriptions, orders, stock, billing, reports and automatic WhatsApp messages will be a second project, so the website can launch first.You asked for these in the form; they're a much bigger job.Later phase
  2. No "Virtual try-on" button yet. It appears once the try-on feature is built, so customers never tap a button that doesn't work.Later phase
  3. Call and WhatsApp ask "Which branch?" first, because each branch has its own number.Website
  4. Favourites are saved on the customer's phone, with no login or account.Website
  5. Sold-out frames stay on the site with a "Sold out" label, as you asked.Website
  6. Staff add frames; an admin approves them before customers see them. Staff can hide a frame, but only the admin can delete one.Your form said staff can delete; this keeps a safety net. Is that OK?Website
  7. Changes to an approved frame go live straight away (for example a new price or "Sold out"), without a second approval. Every change shows in the admin's "Recent changes".Website
  8. The years count automatically from 1999 ("27 Years of Trust" this year), so it's never out of date.Website
  9. Eye care lists your 4 services: eye testing, instant lens fitting for spherical powers, frame adjustment and frame repair. Contact lenses, reading glasses and care products are left out until you confirm you sell them.Website
  10. Reviews: we'll show reviews you collect from customers (with their permission). To show Google reviews, each branch first needs a free Google Business Profile, which we recommend creating now; it also helps people find you on Google Maps.Website
  11. Changing a booking: customers call or WhatsApp the branch. Online cancelling comes with the shop system.Later phase
  12. Visitor statistics (Google Analytics) come later, after a cookie notice is added.Later phase
  13. One Contact page for both branches, instead of a separate page for each.Website
