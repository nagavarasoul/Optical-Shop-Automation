Logo concepts: pick your favourite

VS Lens Mart · Five first drafts · 23 October 2026

How to choose

Each concept is shown on a light background, on navy, and at the small sizes it will appear in the website header, on a phone and in the browser tab. Tell us the number you like best on WhatsApp, along with anything you'd change. We'll then refine that one into the final logo.

1 Lens monogram

"VS" in gold inside a navy lens, with a brass rim and a small highlight, like light catching a lens. Simple, premium and easy to read at any size.

2 Spectacles

A pair of round glasses with "V" in one lens and "S" in the other. It says "optical shop" at a glance.

3 Family eye

An eye with a heart in its centre: eye care given with the warmth of a family shop. Paired with "The Optical Family".

4 Heritage badge

A cleaner version of your current round badge, keeping "Care · Love · Quality · Family" (now spelled correctly) and "Est. 1999". Its ring text is too small to read in the browser tab, so concept 1 would stand in there.

5 Clear vision

A bold "VS" under a gold arc, like the curve of a lens or an eyebrow. The most modern and minimal of the five.
