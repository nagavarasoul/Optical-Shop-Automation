Your eye test

What happens at your eye test

  1. We talk

    We ask about your eyes, your work and any glasses you use now.

  2. We test

    We check your vision for distance and near.

  3. We explain

    We explain your prescription in plain words.

  4. You choose

    We help you choose lenses and frames, with exact prices.

Bring your current glasses and any old prescription. DUMMY (C17): optometrist to review

Good to know

When to get your eyes checked

  • Distance or reading looks blurred
  • Headaches after reading or screen work
  • You find yourself squinting
  • It's been a while since your last eye test
  • A child holds books or phones very close

DUMMY (C17): optometrist to review and approve this list

Need urgent help?

Sudden loss of vision, eye pain, flashes of light or an eye injury? See a doctor or hospital straight away.

Everyday habits

Eye care tips

Take screen breaks

Look away from the screen every so often and rest your eyes.

Read in good light

Use bright, even light for reading and close work.

Wear sunglasses in bright sun

Shade your eyes outdoors on bright days.

Keep your glasses clean

Clean lenses with a soft cloth, not your shirt.

Regular check-ups

Children and older family members benefit from regular eye tests.

Don't ignore changes

If your vision changes, come in for a test.
